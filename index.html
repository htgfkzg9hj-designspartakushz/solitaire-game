How to play
1. Flip one card at a time.
2. Cards are added to the playing area.
3. Only four specific combinations can be selected.
4. Selected cards must total 10, 20, or 30.
5. Press Discard Selected to remove the cards.
6. Continue until all 52 cards are flipped.

Allowed combinations
1. First + Second + Third
2. First + Second + Last
3. First + Last + Second-to-Last
4. Last Three Cards

Card values
Ace = 1.
Cards 2–9 = face value.
10, Jack, Queen, King = 10.

Winning
You win if no cards remain, or if exactly one card remains and its value is 10.
